# Curiouser Kids — AI Photos & Videos Management

## What is this?

Curiouser Kids is an AI-powered app designed for parents of children aged 0–6 years old.

It automatically filters, organizes, and transforms everyday moments—photos and short videos—into meaningful developmental insights.

Instead of manually sorting through thousands of mixed photos in your phone, the app intelligently identifies your child’s moments, curates them, and turns them into:

* Structured memory albums
* AI-generated highlight videos
* Developmental reports based on the EYFS framework

---

## Core Features

### 1. Automatic Photo & Video Filtering

* Detects and extracts children’s photos and videos directly from phone albums
* Eliminates the need for manual selection and uploading
* Keeps all child-related memories in one dedicated space

---

### 2. AI Memory Creation

* Automatically generates:

  * Highlight videos (e.g. Music Class, Playtime, At Home)
  * Smart albums categorized by activities and environments
* Transforms unstructured content into curated, meaningful memories

---

### 3. AI Development Assessment (EYFS-Based)

Using captured photos and videos, the system analyzes and generates reports across the 7 areas of learning and development:

* Physical Development
* Communication & Language
* Personal, Social & Emotional Development
* Literacy
* Mathematics
* Understanding the World
* Expressive Arts & Design

Parents receive clear insights into progress, strengths, and developmental patterns over time.

---

## Why This Exists

After 7 years of running an early years education company, one insight became clear:

Parents capture everything, but struggle to use it meaningfully.

* Thousands of photos and videos are taken
* Content is mixed with daily life media
* Nothing is structured, tracked, or interpreted

This app solves that problem by:

* Organizing children’s memories automatically
* Making them easy to revisit and enjoy
* Turning them into actionable developmental insights

---

## Vision

To transform everyday parenting moments into a continuous, intelligent understanding of a child’s growth.

The most valuable developmental data already exists—it is simply unstructured and underutilized.
